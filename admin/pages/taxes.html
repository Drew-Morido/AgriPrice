Quarterly review
The MFN rice tariff is not fixed. Under EO 105 s.2025 and IATRGA Circular No. 2025-001 it's reviewed every quarter and moves within a 15%–35% band based on the Vietnam 5%-broken price (FAO) vs. a March-2025 baseline.
The DA posts a certification each quarter and BOC issues a CMO — add that confirmed rate below once published.

VAT exemption
Rice is VAT‑exempt as an agricultural product in its original state (NIRC §109), so the 12% VAT should not be added to rice.
