Taxes & Import Charges (imported rice)

Import charges apply to imported rice and raise the price paid by consumers. Rice is VAT-exempt (agricultural product in original state, NIRC §109). The rice catalog and price ranges shown to the public are on the public Rice Catalog page.

Rice Import Tariff (quarterly, price-indexed)

The MFN rice tariff is not fixed. Under EO 105 s.2025 and IAGRTA Circular No. 2025-001 it is reviewed every quarter and moves within a 15%–35% band based on the Vietnam 5%-broken price (FAO) vs a March-2025 baseline. The DA posts a certification each quarter and BOC issues a Customs Memorandum Order — enter that confirmed rate below when published.
