Import tariff (imported rice only). The MFN rice tariff is not fixed: it is reviewed quarterly and price-indexed within a 15–35% band under EO 105 s.2025 & IAGRTA Circular 2025-001 (reduced from RA 11203's 35% by EO 62 s.2024; 15% for Q1 2026). The system tracks each confirmed quarter on the admin Taxes & Import Charges page (effective-date schedule), so this factor uses the rate applicable to each date. It raises the landed cost of imported rice. Measured against retail imported well-milled price (2019–2026), the raw Pearson correlation is −0.03 (negligible): the tariff is a policy step-variable whose retail effect is confounded by global prices and the USD/PHP rate (mean price was even slightly higher at 15% than at 35%, during 2024–25 inflation). It is therefore included as a contextual cost factor for imported rice only, not as a strong linear predictor. Rice is VAT-exempt (NIRC §109), so no VAT is applied. Sources: USDA FAS RP2026-0004; EO 62 s.2024; RA 11203.
