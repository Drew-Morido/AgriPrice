Help

Questions, answers,
and how to reach us

A short FAQ for vendors and households, plus a direct line if something still isn’t clear.

Is AgriPricePH free to use?

Yes. Today’s prices and the short forecast are open with no login. A free account unlocks price history and charts.

Where do the prices come from?

Category prices follow Department of Agriculture monitoring. We also use fuel and USD/PHP signals. We do not invent stall prices.

Why do some grades show brands and others don’t?

DA prices rice by milling grade, not by sack name. Verified NCR listings appear when we have a cited source; other chips are typical stall names.

Is Sinandomeng a seed variety?

Usually not. It is often a market name. Farmers plant registered lines such as NSIC Rc222 or Rc160.

How far ahead is the forecast?

The LSTM estimates the next two days. Treat it as a planning aid, not a guarantee.

Who should I contact about official rice policy?

AgriPricePH is not a DA office. Use the Department of Agriculture Contact Us link in the card beside this list.
